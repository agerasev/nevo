# Runnable Rust baseline

**November 2026.** Establish a repeatable Rust ecosystem connecting recurrent
control, movement, food, energy, reproduction, mutation, and death.

Short seeded runs reproduced feeding, births, turnover, and extinction without
immigration. Viewer observation and checkpoint resume preserved the tested
trajectories. The baseline used sensing before movement, feeding, and births.

This established a runnable foundation on the tested build and platform, not
measured evolutionary improvement. Immigration meant population survival alone
could not demonstrate adaptation.
